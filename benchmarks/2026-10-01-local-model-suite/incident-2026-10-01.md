# Host panic during the Qwen3.5 benchmark

## Observed sequence

- The panic record's calendar time is 2026-10-01 14:45:21 CDT; macOS booted again at 14:45:50 CDT.
- The kernel reported that `watchdogd` had not checked in for 91 seconds. The same report says the VM compressor reached 100% of its segments limit, with 41 swapfiles.
- The panic snapshot lists PID 1591 (`openkind-bench`) at 15,268,588,560 resident bytes and PID 12424 (`openkind_backends-8916f7b4ceb40`) at 31,629,057,040 resident bytes on a 36 GiB host.
- The campaign log shows Qwen3.5 in the 777-row `repeated_full` strategy when logging stopped. There is no completion summary for this score run. The host restart removed the disposable model directory under `/tmp`.

## Assessment

The simultaneous model workloads and VM pressure make combined memory pressure a likely contributor to the watchdog panic. The panic report does not prove which process caused the kernel stall, and this is not evidence of a Qwen3.5 quality failure. The interrupted timing run is incomplete and must not be recorded as a benchmark result.

## Recovery

Keep the completed Qwen3.5 dataset-evaluation artifacts. Resume from verified reports, use a resumable status file, and remove each profile after its remaining runs. Use the documented smoke workload for large controls; reserve the 777-row control run for Qwen3 0.6B and qualified MLX family paths. Run only one model profile at a time.
